01 / geometry

Same mesh, different renderer

What you exported from Blender is drawing here in real time through WebGL. No frames were rendered ahead of time. Rotate it and the shading recalculates every frame.

02 / lighting

Lights don't travel in the file

A .glb carries geometry, materials and textures. It carries no lighting at all. Everything you see here comes from an environment map generated in the browser, which is why swapping that one line changes the entire look.

03 / motion

Two kinds of animation

Keyframes made in Blender ride along inside the file and play back on a mixer. The camera move you're triggering right now was never in Blender. It's reading your scroll position and nothing else.
